## Long Description

### English (4000 chars max)

**Stop revenge trading. Stop overtrading. Lock yourself out.**

PipLock AI is the trading discipline app built for serious traders who know their rules but struggle to follow them. Set your daily limits, activate a challenge, and PipLock physically overlays your broker app the moment you try to break them.

**HOW IT WORKS**
1. Set your trading rules: profit target, max daily loss, max drawdown, max trades per session
2. Activate a challenge (funded account, prop firm, or personal)
3. PipLock monitors your session in the background
4. The moment you hit your limit — a fullscreen overlay locks your broker app
5. You can't trade past your rules. The discipline is enforced.

**FEATURES**

🔒 **Killswitch** — Fullscreen overlay that physically blocks your broker app (MetaTrader 5, MT4) when you reach your daily loss or trade limit. No willpower required.

⚡ **FOMO Gatekeeper** — Before you open your broker app, PipLock makes you confirm you have a valid reason to trade. Forces intentional action.

🤖 **AI Trading Planner** — Generate a personalized trading plan for the day using AI. Chat with your AI coach about your trading mindset, rules, and discipline.

📊 **Challenge Tracker** — Set up funded account challenges (prop firm rules) or personal challenges. Track profit/loss, drawdowns, and winning streaks over time.

📈 **Trade Limit Alerts** — Orange banner when you approach your daily trade limit. Visual friction before you break your rules.

🔔 **Smart Notifications** — Market open/close alerts, session reminders, daily check-ins.

**WHO IS IT FOR**
- Prop firm traders following strict funded account rules
- Retail traders struggling with revenge trading or overtrading
- Anyone who wants to enforce their own trading system with hard stops

**AI FEATURES (Pro)**
The AI coach is powered by a secure server-side proxy — your conversation is never stored permanently and the API key is never exposed in the app.

Pro users get 3 AI plan generations + 20 chat messages per day.
Trial users get 2 + 5 per day (7-day free trial included).

**TECHNICAL NOTE**
PipLock uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect when you open a broker app and show the overlay. It reads only the foreground app's package name — it does not read screen content, keystrokes, or any data from your broker account.

The "Display over other apps" permission is required to show the Killswitch overlay above your broker app.

**NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE**
PipLock AI is a discipline tool. It does not provide trading signals, investment advice, or account management. You are solely responsible for your trading decisions.
